Our Heritage

More Than Food.
A Heritage.

Born from the kitchens of Tamil Nadu, INGU CAFE carries forward a legacy of recipes passed down through generations. Every dish is a conversation between tradition and the present moment.

We source the freshest ingredients, grind our batters fresh each morning, and prepare every dish with the same care a grandmother would. This is not just a cafe — it is a living memory of South India.

— The INGU Family
